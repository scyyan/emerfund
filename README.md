# emerfund.github.io
EMERGENCY FUND TRACKER
======================

A simple, private app for tracking your emergency fund: how many months
your savings would cover, your monthly budget, your paychecks, and a
yearly income snapshot.


GETTING STARTED
---------------
1. Keep "Emergency Fund.html" somewhere handy (for example, your Documents folder).
2. Double-click it. It opens in your web browser. There's nothing to install,
   and it works without the internet.
3. Fill in your numbers:
   - Overview: type your emergency fund balance at the top.
   - Budget: set your monthly costs. "Current" is what you spend now;
     "Adjusted" is a leaner version for a real emergency. Rename, add (+ Add line),
     or delete (✕) lines as you like.
   - Paychecks: click "+ Add paycheck" to log your take-home pay.
   - Income: click "+ Add year" to start the yearly snapshot.

Everything saves automatically as you type.


YOUR DATA IS PRIVATE
--------------------
Your numbers are saved only inside your web browser on your computer.
Nothing is sent anywhere.

Because of that:
  - Always open the app in the SAME browser (for example, always Safari or always
    Chrome). A different browser won't see your numbers.
  - Clearing your browser's history/website data can erase them.


BACK UP NOW AND THEN
--------------------
Go to the "Backup & Notes" tab and click "Export backup". A backup file is saved
to your Downloads folder. To restore it, use "Import backup…" on the same tab.
This is also how you move your data to another computer or browser.


TIPS
----
  - Drag the ⋮⋮ handle (or click it and use the arrow keys) to reorder lines.
  - Moving a line in one budget also moves the same line in the other budget.
  - The sun/moon button at the top switches between light and dark mode.
  - In the Income tab, a year named like a Paychecks year (e.g. "2026") pulls its
    net pay from the Paychecks tab automatically. You can type over it.
