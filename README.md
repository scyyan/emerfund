# emerfund.github.io
EMERGENCY FUND TRACKER
======================

A simple, private app for tracking your emergency fund: how many months
your savings would cover, your monthly budget, your paychecks, and a
yearly income snapshot.


GETTING STARTED
---------------
1. Keep "Emergency Fund.html" somewhere handy (for example, your Documents folder).
2. Double-click it. It opens in your web browser. There's nothing to install,
   and it works without the internet.
3. A short setup asks for your balance, your essential monthly costs and a goal.
   You can skip it and fill things in yourself:
   - Overview: type your emergency fund balance under the chart, and set your
     savings target beside it (in money or months). The target appears on the
     climb as a checkered finish line. After that, click "Add money" or
     "Take out" whenever money moves in or out; each one is saved in your
     Activity and drawn on your progress chart.
   - Your progress: move the "Saving each month" slider to see when you'd
     reach your finish line or next camp. Point at the chart to read any date.
   - Budget: set your costs. "Current" is what you spend now; "Adjusted" is a
     leaner version for a real emergency (leave it blank to match Current).
     Rename, add (+ Add line), or delete (✕) lines as you like.
   - Paychecks: click "Fill the year…" to add a whole year on your pay schedule,
     or "+ Add paycheck" for one at a time. When a payday passes, click "Got it"
     if the amount matched, or type what you received.
   - Income: click "+ Add year" to start the yearly snapshot.

Everything saves automatically as you type.


YOUR DATA IS PRIVATE
--------------------
Your numbers are saved only inside your web browser on your computer.
Nothing is sent anywhere.

Because of that:
  - Always open the app in the SAME browser (for example, always Safari or always
    Chrome). A different browser won't see your numbers.
  - Clearing your browser's history/website data can erase them.


BACK UP NOW AND THEN
--------------------
Go to the "Backup & Notes" tab and click "Export backup". A backup file is saved
to your Downloads folder. To restore it, use "Import backup…" on the same tab.
This is also how you move your data to another computer or browser.


TIPS
----
  - Drag the ⋮⋮ handle (or click it and use the arrow keys) to reorder lines.
  - Pay a bill yearly or every 3 months? Switch the line's /mo to /yr or /qtr and
    type the bill as it comes. Totals are always shown per month.
  - Deleted something by mistake? Click "Undo" in the bar at the bottom.
  - On Overview, click a row under Camps to show that level on the climb.
  - Point at (or click) a camp flag, your orange pin or the Adjusted ring on the
    climb to see the numbers behind it. Drag the checkered finish flag to move
    your goal; with no goal yet, drag the faint flag.
  - The app nudges you when your balance hasn't been updated in a month, or when
    a backup is due.
  - The sun/moon button at the top switches between light and dark mode.
  - In the Income tab, a year named like a Paychecks year (e.g. "2026") pulls its
    net pay from the Paychecks tab automatically. You can type over it.
