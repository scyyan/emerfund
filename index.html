Emergency Fund

How to use E-Fund

A simple, private app for tracking your emergency fund: how many months your savings would cover, your monthly budget, your paychecks, and a yearly income snapshot.

Install it on your iPhone

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap Share → Add to Home Screen → Add.
  3. Open it from the Home Screen. After the first time, it works without internet.

Getting started

  • Overview: type your emergency fund balance at the top, and pick your currency with the buttons beside it.
  • Budget: set your monthly costs. "Current" is what you spend now; "Adjusted" is a leaner version for a real emergency. Rename, add (+ Add line) or delete (the X button) lines as you like.
  • Paychecks: tap "+ Add paycheck" to log your take-home pay.
  • Income: tap "+ Add year" to start the yearly snapshot.

Everything saves automatically as you type.

Your data is private

  • Your numbers are saved only on this device, inside this app. They are never sent anywhere, and other people using this site can't see them.
  • Each device keeps its own copy. Your phone and your computer don't sync; use Export/Import to move data between them.
  • The Home Screen app and the same page opened in Safari don't share data either. Add it to your Home Screen first, then enter your numbers there (or use Export/Import to carry them over).
  • Deleting the app from your Home Screen, or clearing Safari's website data, erases your numbers. Back up now and then.

Back up and restore

  • Go to Backup & Notes → Export backup. On a phone, choose Save to Files or AirDrop; on a computer it goes to Downloads.
  • To restore, or to move your data to another device, use Import backup… on the same tab.

Tips

  • Reorder lines with the up/down arrow buttons on a phone, or drag the dotted handle on a computer. Moving a line in one budget also moves the same line in the other.
  • The two-arrow button copies a line into the other budget.
  • The currency buttons only change the symbol shown; they don't convert amounts between currencies.
  • The sun/moon button switches between light and dark mode.
  • On the Income tab, a year named like a Paychecks year (e.g. "2026") pulls its net pay from the Paychecks tab automatically. You can type over it.
