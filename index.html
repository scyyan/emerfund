Emergency Fund

How to use E-Fund

A simple, private app for tracking your emergency fund: how many months your savings would cover, your monthly budget, your paychecks, and a yearly income snapshot.

Install it on your iPhone

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap Share → Add to Home Screen → Add.
  3. Open it from the Home Screen. After the first time, it works without internet.

Getting started

The first time you open the app, a short setup asks for your currency, your balance, your biggest monthly costs and a goal. You can skip it and fill things in yourself.

  • Overview: type your emergency fund balance under the chart, and pick your currency: on a computer use the buttons beside the balance, on a phone tap the symbol just before it. After that, tap Add money or Take out whenever money moves in or out. Each one is saved in your Activity and drawn on your progress chart.
  • Your progress: the chart shows your balance over time. Touch it to read any point. Drag the Saving each month slider to see when you'd reach your finish line or next camp.
  • Target: set your savings goal beside the balance, in money or in months (tap $ or mo). It shows up on the climb as a checkered finish line. You can also drag that flag left or right to move your goal; with no goal yet, drag the faint flag.
  • Budget: set your costs. "Current" is what you spend now; "Adjusted" is a leaner version for a real emergency. Leave Adjusted blank to keep it the same as Current. Rename, add (+ Add line) or delete (the X button) lines as you like. A bar pinned to the bottom shows how many months you're covered as you edit.
  • Paychecks: tap "Fill the year…" to add a whole year on your pay schedule, or "+ Add paycheck" for one at a time. When a payday passes, tap Got it if the amount matched, or type what you received.
  • Other income: below the paychecks, tap "+ Add income source" for side gigs, rent from a roommate or anything else that comes in. Enter the take-home amount, how often it arrives (week, month or year), and when it started. It's added on the Income tab for the part of each year it was active.
  • Income: tap "+ Add year" to start the yearly snapshot.

Everything saves automatically as you type.

What the numbers mean

  • Months covered: your balance divided by a month of costs. If your income stopped today, that's how long your fund would last.
  • Necessities only, + Subs, + All Expenses: which costs count. Necessities are the must-pays (housing, food, insurance); + Subs adds subscriptions and other recurring bills; + All Expenses adds everything else.
  • Current and Adjusted: Current is how you spend now. Adjusted is a leaner budget you'd switch to in an emergency, so it stretches further; it's the hollow ring on the climb.
  • Camps: 3, 6, 9 and 12 months covered. Many people aim for 3 to 6.
  • Monthly discretionary (Income tab): a month of paychecks plus other income, minus your Current budget. It's what's left to save each month, and it's the starting amount for Saving each month until the app has a couple of months of your deposits to go on.
  • Disposable (Income tab): the same idea for a whole year, after the extra + and − lines you add.
  • Right after setup, the months are based on only the few costs you entered. Add the rest on the Budget tab for the real number.

Your data is private

  • Your numbers are saved only on this device, inside this app. They are never sent anywhere, and other people using this site can't see them.
  • Each device keeps its own copy. Your phone and your computer don't sync; use Export/Import to move data between them.
  • The Home Screen app and the same page opened in Safari don't share data either. Add it to your Home Screen first, then enter your numbers there (or use Export/Import to carry them over).
  • Deleting the app from your Home Screen, or clearing Safari's website data, erases your numbers. Back up now and then.

Back up and restore

  • Go to Backup & Notes → Export backup. On a phone, choose Save to Files or AirDrop; on a computer it goes to Downloads.
  • To restore, or to move your data to another device, use Import backup… on the same tab.

Tips

  • Bills you pay yearly or every 3 months? Switch the line's /mo to /yr or /qtr and type the bill as it comes. Totals are always shown per month.
  • Deleted something by mistake? Tap Undo in the bar that appears at the bottom (tap it again to undo the change before). On a computer, Cmd+Z or Ctrl+Z works too. Deleted lines, paychecks and money entries also wait 30 days under Recently deleted on the Backup & Notes tab.
  • On Overview, tap a row under Camps to show that level on the climb.
  • Tap a camp flag, your orange pin or the Adjusted ring on the climb to see the numbers behind it.
  • The app gives you a gentle nudge when your balance hasn't been updated in a month, or when a backup is due.
  • Reorder lines with the up/down arrow buttons on a phone, or drag the dotted handle on a computer.
  • The currency buttons only change the symbol shown; they don't convert amounts between currencies.
  • The sun/moon button switches between light and dark mode.
  • On the Income tab, a year named like a Paychecks year (e.g. "2026") pulls its net pay from the Paychecks tab automatically. You can type over it.
